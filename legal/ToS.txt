## 5. ENCRYPTION: REGULAR CHATS AND SECRET CHATS

### 5.1. Regular Chats

Messages in regular private chats, groups and channels are encrypted in transit and stored encrypted on NiosMess servers. However, this is **server-side encryption**: the encryption keys are held by the Service. This is **not** end-to-end encryption. The Administration therefore has the technical ability to access the content of regular chats, and may do so only where reasonably necessary to operate and secure the Service, investigate reports and abuse, protect users, or comply with applicable law. Do not use regular chats for information that must be unreadable by the Service.

### 5.2. Secret Chats

Secret Chats use end-to-end encryption for supported message content. Where correctly implemented and used, the content is intended to be readable only by the participants' devices. The Administration does not intentionally keep the ability to decrypt properly encrypted Secret Chat content using only the data stored on its servers.

### 5.3. Limits of E2EE

End-to-end encryption does not hide all technical data. Information needed for operation, delivery, abuse prevention and account security (for example account identifiers, timing, participants and message size) may exist separately from encrypted content. Secret Chats are tied to device-specific keys and may not be available on every device or client. The characteristics of Secret Chats may change as the Service develops.

### 5.4. Access to Secret Chat Content

The Administration may receive Secret Chat content where a participant reports a message, forwards content to moderators, the content is available on a participant's own device, or the content is otherwise supplied to the Service outside the encrypted conversation. The Administration does not proactively inspect properly encrypted Secret Chat content, but may act on evidence supplied by a participant or that otherwise lawfully becomes available.

### 5.5. Loss of Keys

The Administration is not responsible for the inability to access encrypted content caused by device loss or reset, deletion of local keys, session termination, reinstalling the application, use of a key reset feature, or corruption of local storage. Secret Chat content cannot be recovered by the Administration.

## 6. NIOSAI AND AI FEATURES

### 6.1. What NiosAI Is

NiosAI Lite is an AI assistant built into NiosMess. It generates answers automatically and may be used in a direct chat with the bot or, where enabled, by mentioning `@ai` or `@nios_ai` in a group or channel.

### 6.2. Group and Channel Use

Use of `@ai` in a group or channel is **off by default** and can be enabled or disabled by the chat's administrators. When it is enabled, messages that mention the assistant, and their AI replies, are visible to all members of that chat. Only the person who asked can use the buttons on their own AI reply.

### 6.3. Quota

AI usage is measured in **characters** (your prompt, the system instructions sent with it, any short conversation context, and the reply) within a sliding **72-hour window**. The current allowance is 50,000 characters for regular Users and 200,000 characters with an active Nios+ subscription. When the allowance is used up, the assistant stops answering until the window resets. Limits may change, and the Service shows the current values.

### 6.4. Third-Party Processing

To answer, the text you send to NiosAI (and short recent context for that chat) is transmitted to a third-party AI provider, currently Polza.ai, which routes requests to a language model. Do not send to NiosAI anything you would not want processed by such a provider. NiosAI conversations are not end-to-end encrypted and must not be used in Secret Chats. Short conversation context is kept only temporarily in the Service's memory and is cleared on request or when the Service restarts.

### 6.5. Accuracy

AI output can be wrong, incomplete, outdated or inappropriate. NiosAI is not a source of professional medical, legal, financial or safety advice. You are responsible for how you use its output, and the Administration is not liable for decisions made in reliance on it.

### 6.6. Identity

NiosAI Lite is a NiosMess assistant. It is not a human and does not speak for the Administration.
